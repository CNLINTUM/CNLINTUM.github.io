Privacy policy

This application is a personal rclone configuration used only by its owner to copy his own files to and from his own Google Drive.

  • It requests only the drive.file scope: access to files the application itself created.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data with anyone.
  • All data stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's own computers.

Last updated: 26 October 2026.
